HERITAGE SINCE 1890

Over a Century of Trust in Villivakkam

Passed down through four generations, Mohan Jewellery stands as a beacon of craftsmanship, authenticity, and customer devotion.

1890

Founding by Sri Kota Munirathnam Chetty

Sri Kota Munirathnam Chetty established the original goldsmith studio in Palani Andavar Koil Street, Villivakkam, laying the foundation of unyielding purity and trust.

1940s

Second Generation & Temple Guild Mastery

Expanded into handcrafted South Indian temple jewellery, supplying sacred ornaments to grand temples across Greater Chennai.

1990s

Pioneering BIS 916 Hallmarking

Among the first jewellery showrooms in Villivakkam to adopt Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) hallmarking, guaranteeing 100% gold purity.

2026

4th Generation Bespoke Luxury Studio

Today, Mohan Jewellery blends 134 years of family heritage with modern bespoke design, conflict-free diamond certification, and digital interactive services.
